Slow Cooker Broccoli Beef

Description

Tender, juicy beef strips and broccoli are coated in a savory garlic sauce.

Several home cooks recommend adding broccoli directly to the slow cooker to save time and cleanup.

Ingredients

  • 1 beef bouillon cube
  • 1 cup warm water
  • ½ cup reduced-sodium soy sauce, or to taste
  • ⅓ cup brown sugar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 ½ pounds beef sirloin, cut into 1-inch strips
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 2 pounds broccoli florets, or to taste
  • 2 cups cooked jasmine rice
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds, for garnish (optional)
